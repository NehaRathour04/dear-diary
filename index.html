Under the Hood

Military-Grade Encryption,
Actually Explained

No buzzwords. Here's exactly how your data is protected.

PBKDF2

Key Derivation

Your password is put through PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations, SHA-256) to generate a strong AES key. Brute-force attacks become computationally infeasible.

AES-256-GCM

Content Encryption

A fresh random AES-256 key encrypts your entry content. GCM mode also authenticates the data — any tampering is immediately detected and rejected.

RSA-2048 OAEP

Key Exchange

RSA-OAEP wraps each entry's AES key. Your public key is stored plaintext; your private key is encrypted with AES-256 before storage — even we can't access it.

Web Crypto API

Browser-Native

All cryptographic operations run in your browser using the built-in Web Crypto API. There are no third-party crypto libraries to trust or audit.

⚠️

Important: Because your password is never stored, forgetting it means losing your entries permanently. There is no backdoor. Choose a strong password and keep it safe.
